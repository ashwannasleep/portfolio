# 🔍 How to Find Your Apple Developer Team ID

## Quick Steps

1. **Go to Apple Developer Portal**
   - Visit: https://developer.apple.com/account/
   - Log in with your Apple Developer account

2. **Go to Membership Page**
   - Click on **"Membership"** in the left sidebar
   - Or go directly to: https://developer.apple.com/account/#/membership/

3. **Find Team ID**
   - On the Membership page, you'll see your account information
   - Look for **"Team ID"** or **"Team"** section
   - The Team ID is usually displayed as: `ABC123DEF4` (10 characters, letters and numbers)
   - It might be labeled as:
     - "Team ID"
     - "Team"
     - Or shown next to your organization/individual name

## Visual Guide

The Team ID typically appears:
- At the top of the Membership page
- Next to your name/organization
- In a section labeled "Team Information"
- Format: 10 characters (e.g., `7Z45F36LFG`)

## Alternative: Check Your Account Info

If you can't find it on the Membership page:
1. Look at the top-right corner of the Apple Developer portal
2. Your Team ID might be shown next to your name
3. Or check the URL when you're logged in - sometimes it's in the URL parameters

## What It Looks Like

- Format: `ABC123DEF4` (10 alphanumeric characters)
- Usually all uppercase
- Example: `7Z45F36LFG` or `ABC123DEF4`
